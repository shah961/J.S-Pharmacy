Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Introduction

J.S Pharmacy ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to our website and describes how we handle information when you visit our static site.

2. Information We May Receive

Because this website is a static informational site, we do not automatically gather or store personal health records or login details. If you contact us via telephone or email, we only use the information you provide to fulfill your direct request.

3. Contact Form Information

Our website contact form operates purely as a client-side interface to prepare communications. The website itself does not store your form submissions in a central database unless explicit backend form processing integrations are connected in the future.

4. How Information Is Used

Any information communicated directly to us (via phone or direct email) is strictly used to address your product or service enquiries regarding our Paragon City store.

5. Cookies & Tracking

This static website does not employ tracking cookies, profiling cookies, or invasive behavioral monitoring technologies.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may provide external hyperlinked directions (such as Google Maps). These external services operate independently under their respective privacy terms.

7. Data Security

We exercise standard caution regarding any communication received directly via email or telephone. However, visitors should avoid transmitting sensitive health conditions over general online contact channels.

8. Data Retention & Children's Privacy

We do not collect personal identifiers from children or store user profiles on this platform.

9. Your Rights

You may request the deletion or clarification of any communication sent directly to our email address at any time.
