About J.S Pharmacy

Your trusted partner in healthcare, wellness, and community support in Paragon City, Lahore.

Our Purpose

Serving Paragon City with Integrity & Care

Situated right on Barki Road opposite Gate No. 2 (Block D), J.S Pharmacy was founded to deliver reliable, genuine, and accessible healthcare solutions to the growing community of Paragon City and surrounding areas.

We prioritize quality over everything else. Every product on our shelves—from vital daily prescriptions and over-the-counter medicines to specialized baby care items and skincare line—is sourced meticulously to ensure authenticity and efficacy.

Whether you need urgent medication support, routine wellness check supplies, or simple personal care advice, our store is structured to make your visits swift, comfortable, and informative.
